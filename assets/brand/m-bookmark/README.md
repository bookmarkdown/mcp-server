## Direction

Only M appears in the icon. The bookmark silhouette provides the background; soft parallel curves form a quiet texture. A clear space around the rounded M keeps the letter legible. The palette uses ivory and warm ink.

## Assets

* `m-bookmark.svg`: ivory bookmark with ink M and subtle curved texture.
* `m-bookmark-dark.svg`: ink bookmark with ivory M and subtle curved texture.
* `m-bookmark-plain.svg`: simple ink bookmark without texture for small applications.
* `preview.png`: large and small previews.

SVGs have transparent outer backgrounds and use self-contained vector paths and clipping without fonts or external resources. Prefer the plain mark below 64 px. This design option is supplied separately and is not applied to the server interface.
